✍️ Primordial Mind and the First Creation

🟡 When the first energy manifests, the birth of duality appears accordingly.

☯  : [()λOb → ()f∞]

🔘 The interplay between darkness and light takes the form of a frequency()hz, and these two reinforce one another. This is called "balance."

Yin and yang, as the first birth, are therefore the cause of the creation of all things.

Yang is clear and manifest. Yin is concealed and within.

Within yin there is yang as its core; within yang there is yin as its core. This is the same, for the sake of self-examining the state of balance.

☯  Yin and yang that are perfectly pure share one singularity, just as the mind does. They exist in the first birth. Can there be a second?

Cultivating the mind differs from "the first birth of yin and yang," because a person can access it in a state of complete observation: stillness, the observer.

✍️ The Normal State of Yin and Yang: Yang Within Yin, Yin Within Yang

👫 Women and men naturally have distinct characteristics shaped by two factors: physical form and life purpose. In women, femininity is more pronounced because of the capacity to bear children and the ability to endure physical and emotional pain. Men are more often inclined toward the qualities of the yang principle.

Balanced yin and yang do not refer to femininity or masculinity, but to a person who possesses both action and thought, clearly oriented toward creation.

In the natural order of men, few are distinctly yang, even with suitable means and capacity. This is because society grants men more acceptance as leaders, so yin and yang cannot fully manifest. In truth, this shows up in distorted speech, secrecy on the dark side, allowing others to control one's power, and other behaviors that do not reflect clear, yang-like character.

☀️ The sun is a clear expression of yang. It is the source of energy for the earth and all things. 🔥 Even when covered by rain clouds or the darkness of night, it never stops releasing energy.🌙☆ Its light is reflected onto the moon, which gives light to the dark night.

👣 The negative side of yin arises because yin is hidden and unclear. It is an inner nature, so it affects the natural world more than the decisions of people, organizational leaders, or state leaders.

⏳ Destructive yin is not true yin. Its negative side comes from suspicion and from being out of balance. It lacks a clear foundation in thought and is reversed.

The characteristic of this reversed yin is an effort to distort reality and to make itself prominent. This obstructs the whole system of creation: at the smallest level, in family and friendship; at the middle level, in society and relations between states; and at the largest level, in the destruction of the natural world, even when it appears as good intentions.
